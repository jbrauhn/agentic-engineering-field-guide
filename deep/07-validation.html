Verification & Observability

How it works

Definitions

Proof defines required evidence. Verification creates/evaluates tests, checks, reviews, evals, and observations. Observability makes system evidence available. Validation is the gate that determines whether the outcome satisfies the Contract.

Contract Proof
Planned verification
Execution evidence
Independent Validation
Route result

Independence

The executor cannot rewrite Proof, suppress failure, or control the final judge. Use as much independence as risk warrants.

How we work

During Planning

Map each load-bearing Proof item to credible tests/evals/reviews, environment, fixtures, thresholds, and evidence collection.

During Execution

Run fast feedback continuously: unit/integration tests, static/security checks, benchmarks, telemetry, and maker-checker where useful.

At Validation

Evaluate evidence independently and route: accept; execution defect/retry; Plan defect/replan; Contract-change proposal; or uncertain evidence/escalation.

Quality

Include human-rated mission/user quality and independent engineering quality where automation cannot fully judge the outcome.
