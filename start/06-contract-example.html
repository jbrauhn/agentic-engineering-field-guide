Contract for an internal task app

A greenfield task-tracking app for about 500 employees, replacing spreadsheets, hosted on Azure with Microsoft Entra ID.

Goal

Desired outcome

Deliver a secure internal task-tracking application that replaces spreadsheet-based coordination.

Spec

What must be true

Create, assign, reassign, and complete tasks; email reminders; Azure deployment; Entra ID. Explicit non-goals keep adjacent scope out.

Proof

Evidence of success

Functional and security evidence, staging deployment, performance target, and representative Product Owner acceptance scenarios.

Human: articulate, correct, choose. AI: interview, structure, challenge, expose assumptions, draft.
