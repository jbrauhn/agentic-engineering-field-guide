Why this is an engineering discipline

In the past, AI efforts often centered on getting a model to produce a good answer. Agentic Engineering asks a broader question: How do we engineer a system that repeatedly produces trustworthy outcomes?

The AI is a component

Model capability matters, but reliability also depends on context, tools, architecture, controls, feedback, and verification.

The lifecycle matters

Products must survive features, defects, upgrades, operations, security work, modernization, and retirement.

Repeatability matters

Professional work needs inspectable decisions, recoverable failures, versioned artifacts, and evidence.

The human role moves upward

AI performs more execution; humans concentrate on intent, understanding, tradeoffs, creativity, risk, authority, and accountability.
