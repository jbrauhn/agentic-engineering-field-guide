AGENTIC ENGINEERING FIELD GUIDE

Engineering trustworthy work with AI agents

A progressive guide and reference for understanding and applying Agentic Engineering: how humans, AI agents, architecture, context, tools, governance, verification, and iterative learning work together across the product lifecycle.

AI changes the scale of engineering—not the need for engineering discipline. Professional teams have spent decades learning how to design products, architect systems, develop software, test behavior, manage risk, secure resources, and deliver iteratively. Agentic Engineering builds on that foundation. Agents can multiply useful work dramatically; they can also multiply defects and poor decisions at the same speed. The goal is to combine AI speed with proven engineering practice so teams can move rapidly without compounding low-quality work.
