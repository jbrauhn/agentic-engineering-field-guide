<div className="quote"><strong>In the past, AI efforts</strong> often centered on getting a model to produce a good answer. Agentic Engineering asks a broader question: <strong>How do we engineer a system that repeatedly produces trustworthy outcomes?</strong></div>

<div className="grid two">
  <div className="card"><h3>The AI is a component</h3><p className="muted">Model capability matters, but reliability also depends on context, tools, architecture, controls, feedback, and verification.</p></div>
  <div className="card"><h3>The lifecycle matters</h3><p className="muted">Products must survive features, defects, upgrades, operations, security work, modernization, and retirement.</p></div>
  <div className="card"><h3>Repeatability matters</h3><p className="muted">Professional work needs inspectable decisions, recoverable failures, versioned artifacts, and evidence.</p></div>
  <div className="card"><h3>The human role moves upward</h3><p className="muted">AI performs more execution; humans concentrate on intent, understanding, tradeoffs, creativity, risk, authority, and accountability.</p></div>
</div>

<div className="callout good"><strong>Engineering discipline is leverage.</strong> Established practices are what let teams exploit agentic speed without compounding low-quality work.</div>
