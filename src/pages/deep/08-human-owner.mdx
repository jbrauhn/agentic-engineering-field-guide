## Human-owned value
Intent, domain and contextual knowledge, logic, ingenuity, creativity, taste, values, consequential judgment, Decision Authority, and accountability.

## Twelve competency areas
1. Domain judgment & taste
2. Problem framing & specification
3. Clarification & requirements reasoning
4. Decomposition & planning
5. Prompt/instruction engineering
6. Context curation
7. Model/tool/resource selection
8. Orchestration & delegation
9. Verification & eval design
10. Review/debugging/trace reading
11. Risk/authority/guardrail design
12. Knowledge capture & feedback flywheel

<div className="quote">Delegation is not abdication.</div>

<span className="pill blue">How we work</span>

## Thinking partnership
AI should interview, challenge, ideate, explain, diagram, compare alternatives, execute delegated work, and surface evidence—not merely wait for commands.

## Human-native communication
Use visual architecture, IG-style explanations, alternatives, consequences, and evidence. The Human Owner should judge surfaced impact, not discover hidden coupling unaided.

## Learning side quests
Either partner can pause delivery to understand a technology, architecture, product idea, or risk. Capture useful conclusions back into the Plan, ADR, knowledge base, experiment record, or handoff.

## Agent support principle
Agents should strengthen human competencies without quietly replacing human agency.
