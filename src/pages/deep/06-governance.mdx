## Five questions
| Dimension | Question |
| --- | --- |
| **Identity** | Who or what is acting? |
| **Access Path** | How can it technically reach the resource? |
| **Entitlement** | What standing rights exist? |
| **Operational Authority (OA)** | What may this actor do in this task/runtime? |
| **Decision Authority (DA)** | Who may approve consequential decisions? |

## Decision vs enforcement
Policy/control logic decides what should be allowed. Policy Enforcement Points (PEPs) at protected boundaries make that decision real.

## Setup
Define reserved Decision Authority, immutable objects, standing entitlements, and high-impact actions.

## Planning
Identify required resources and capabilities and propose the least practical Operational Authority for the work.

## Action time
Evaluate actual actor + task + target + environment + requested action. Permit, constrain, block, or route for approval.

## Evidence
Record policy decision, approval where required, enforcement result, actor identity, target, and task context. Unauthorized Contract mutation fails closed.
