## Definitions
**Proof** defines required evidence. **Verification** creates and evaluates tests, checks, reviews, evals, and observations. **Observability** makes system evidence available. **Validation** is the gate that determines whether the outcome satisfies the Contract.

## Independence
The executor cannot rewrite Proof, suppress failure, or control the final judge. Use as much independence as risk warrants.
</section>
<section className="pane">
<span className="pill blue">How we work</span>

## During Planning
Map each load-bearing Proof item to credible tests, evals, reviews, environment, fixtures, thresholds, and evidence collection.

## During Execution
Run fast feedback continuously: unit/integration tests, static and security checks, benchmarks, telemetry, and Maker → Checker where useful.

## At Validation
Evaluate evidence independently and route: accept; execution defect/retry; Plan defect/replan; Contract-change proposal; or uncertain evidence/escalation.

## Quality
Include human-rated mission/user quality and independent engineering quality where automation cannot fully judge the outcome.
