The system moves human effort away from repetitive execution and toward places where contextual knowledge, logic, ingenuity, creativity, judgment, values, and accountability have the highest value.

<div className="grid four">
  <div className="card"><h3>What it is</h3><p className="muted">Human-owned intent, consequential judgment, and Decision Authority.</p></div>
  <div className="card"><h3>Why it matters</h3><p className="muted">Not every important question is provable: mission value, taste, acceptable risk, ethics, and product tradeoffs often require context.</p></div>
  <div className="card"><h3>Failure prevented</h3><p className="muted">Agency surrender—or the opposite extreme, human micromanagement of every technical step.</p></div>
  <div className="card gold-line"><h3>Core philosophy</h3><p className="muted"><strong>Delegation is not abdication.</strong></p></div>
</div>

<div className="callout good"><strong>This is Human–AI collaboration.</strong> AI is both thinking partner and executor. It should communicate with human-native means—conversation, diagrams, interactive graphical explanations, alternatives, consequences, and evidence—so the human decides from understanding.</div>

<div className="quote">Either partner can initiate a learning side quest to understand a technology, architecture, risk, or product idea. Useful conclusions return to durable state: the Plan, ADR, knowledge base, experiment record, or handoff.</div>
