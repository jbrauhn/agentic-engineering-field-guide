Agentic Engineering does not invent every principle it uses. It combines established software, architecture, security, and delivery disciplines with emerging agentic practices. **Not everything here is a formal standard.**

<div className="grid two">
  <div className="card"><h3>Agile mindset</h3><p className="muted">Working outcomes, collaboration, responsiveness to change, iterative learning.</p><a href="https://agilemanifesto.org/">Agile Manifesto →</a></div>
  <div className="card"><h3>SOLID principles</h3><p className="muted">Design guidance for maintainable, change-tolerant software; review signal, not blind dogma.</p><a href="https://blog.cleancoder.com/uncle-bob/2020/10/18/Solid-Relevance.html">Robert C. Martin →</a></div>
  <div className="card"><h3>Architecture discipline + C4</h3><p className="muted">Architecture views create a shared system model that Plans and decisions can reference.</p><a href="https://c4model.com/">C4 Model →</a></div>
  <div className="card"><h3>Architecture Decision Records</h3><p className="muted">Preserve architecturally significant decisions, rationale, alternatives, and consequences.</p><a href="https://adr.github.io/">ADR community →</a></div>
  <div className="card"><h3>Zero Trust</h3><p className="muted">Resource-focused authorization and enforcement rather than implicit trust.</p><a href="https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/207/final">NIST SP 800-207 →</a></div>
  <div className="card"><h3>AI risk management</h3><p className="muted">Risk-oriented governance for trustworthy AI.</p><a href="https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework">NIST AI RMF →</a></div>
  <div className="card"><h3>MCP + A2A</h3><p className="muted">Interoperability options for tools/context and independent agents; useful options, not requirements.</p><a href="https://modelcontextprotocol.io/">MCP →</a> · <a href="https://a2a-protocol.org/">A2A →</a></div>
  <div className="card"><h3>Observability</h3><p className="muted">Vendor-neutral telemetry can expose logs, metrics, and traces as evidence.</p><a href="https://opentelemetry.io/docs/">OpenTelemetry →</a></div>
  <div className="card"><h3>Simple-first agent design</h3><p className="muted">Start simple; increase orchestration complexity only when task shape or evidence warrants it.</p><a href="https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents">Anthropic →</a></div>
  <div className="card"><h3>Memory & continuity patterns</h3><p className="muted">AGENTS.md-style guidance, structured knowledge, handoffs, session memory, compaction, retrieval, and fresh-agent relays are selectable mechanisms to experiment with.</p><a href="https://openai.com/index/harness-engineering/">OpenAI Harness Engineering →</a></div>
</div>

<div className="callout"><strong>Classification matters:</strong> formal standards/frameworks, established practices, emerging protocols, and AE house patterns should be labeled honestly rather than blended into one authority tier.</div>
