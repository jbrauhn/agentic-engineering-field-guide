Before an agent can choose how to work, the system needs a stable definition of what the work is trying to achieve.

<div className="grid four">
  <div className="card"><h3>What it is</h3><p className="muted">Loop Context plus Goal / Spec / Proof <InfoTip label="Goal Spec Proof">Goal = desired outcome. Spec = requirements, constraints, non-goals, invariants, assumptions, and boundaries. Proof = the evidence required to demonstrate success.</InfoTip></p></div>
  <div className="card"><h3>Why it matters</h3><p className="muted">Agents are very good at making progress—even toward the wrong target.</p></div>
  <div className="card"><h3>Failure prevented</h3><p className="muted">“Seems good” completion and moving acceptance criteria.</p></div>
  <div className="card gold-line"><h3>Core philosophy</h3><p className="muted"><strong>Welcome new information; control mutation.</strong> Agents propose Contract changes; they never silently rewrite an approved target.</p></div>
</div>
