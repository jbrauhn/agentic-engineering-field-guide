The components are not an independent checklist. Each compensates for limits or risks created by the others.

| Relationship | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Contract ↔ Planning** | The Plan can change without changing intent; genuine intent change becomes a Contract proposal. |
| **Planning ↔ Knowledge** | Architecture-aware planning depends on durable architecture/code knowledge and prior decisions. |
| **Planning ↔ Validation** | Proof becomes a verification strategy before the executor sees results. |
| **Execution ↔ Context** | Workers receive bounded fresh context and write back state, evidence, and handoffs. |
| **Execution ↔ Governance** | Agents adapt implementation without gaining new authority. |
| **Execution ↔ Validation** | Execution generates evidence; independent Validation judges it. |
| **Human Owner ↔ every stage** | Human judgment is concentrated at intent, tradeoff, approval, change, and acceptance points. |

<div className="quote">The architecture is not trying to make AI less autonomous. It is trying to make greater autonomy <strong>safe to delegate.</strong></div>
