Loop Context & Contract

Before an agent can choose how to work, the system needs a stable definition of what the work is trying to achieve.

What it is

Loop Context plus Goal / Spec / Proof iGoal = desired outcome. Spec = requirements, constraints, non-goals, invariants, and boundaries. Proof = the evidence required to demonstrate success..

Why it matters

Agents are very good at making progress—even toward the wrong target.

Failure prevented

“Seems good” completion and moving acceptance criteria.

Core philosophy

Welcome new information; control mutation. Agents propose Contract changes; they never silently rewrite an approved target.

Proof is not the whole test plan

Contract Proof

States what evidence must exist for the outcome to be accepted.

Planning verification strategy

Determines how tests, evals, reviews, environments, fixtures, and instrumentation will generate that evidence.
