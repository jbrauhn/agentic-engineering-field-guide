EXPLORE · TECHNICAL ACCESS IS NOT AUTHORITY

Governed Access & Zero Trust

A system becomes dangerous when it confuses “the agent can reach this resource” with “the agent is allowed to make this decision.”

What it is

Identity, access path, entitlement, Operational Authority (OA), Decision Authority (DA), policy decisions, and enforcement.

Why it matters

Agent capability can exceed appropriate authority.

Failure prevented

The “god-agent” with broad standing credentials and no meaningful boundary.

Core philosophy

Access ≠ authority. Policy decision and enforcement are separate.

When does it happen?

Decisions

System/Contract setup reserves consequential Decision Authority. Planning identifies resources and proposes Operational Authority. At action time, the actual actor/task/resource request is evaluated and may require approval.

Enforcement

A Policy Enforcement Point (PEP) iThe control at or near a protected resource boundary that permits, constrains, blocks, or pauses an action according to policy. enforces the decision when the action reaches a protected tool, API, runtime, secret, data boundary, or deployment system.
