EXPLORE · KEEP HUMAN VALUE WHERE IT MATTERS MOST

Human Owner & KSAs

The system moves human effort away from repetitive execution and toward places where contextual knowledge, logic, ingenuity, creativity, judgment, values, and accountability have the highest value.

What it is

Human-owned intent, consequential judgment, and Decision Authority.

Why it matters

Not every important question is provable: mission value, taste, acceptable risk, ethics, and product tradeoffs often require context.

Failure prevented

Agency surrender—or the opposite extreme, human micromanagement of every technical step.

Core philosophy

Delegation is not abdication.

This is Human–AI collaboration. AI is both thinking partner and executor. It should communicate with human-native means—conversation, diagrams, interactive graphical explanations, alternatives, consequences, and evidence—so the human decides from understanding.
Either partner can initiate a learning side quest to understand a technology, architecture, risk, or product idea. Useful conclusions return to durable state: the Plan, ADR, knowledge base, experiment record, or handoff.
