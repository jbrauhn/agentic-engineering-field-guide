Verification & Validation

The executor knows what it intended to do. Validation exists because intention and confident self-report are not enough evidence that the outcome is correct.

What it is

Verification generates evidence; Validation independently determines whether that evidence satisfies Contract Proof.

Why it matters

AI can produce plausible failure: code can compile while violating the requirement.

Failure prevented

Executor-controlled success criteria and suppressed failure.

Core philosophy

The doer should not control the judge.
