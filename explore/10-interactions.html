How the parts reinforce one another

The components are not an independent checklist. Each compensates for limits or risks created by the others.

RelationshipWhy it matters
Contract ↔ PlanningThe Plan can change without changing intent; genuine intent change becomes a Contract proposal.
Planning ↔ KnowledgeArchitecture-aware planning depends on durable architecture/code knowledge and prior decisions.
Planning ↔ ValidationProof becomes a verification strategy before the executor sees results.
Execution ↔ ContextWorkers receive bounded fresh context and write back state, evidence, and handoffs.
Execution ↔ GovernanceAgents adapt implementation without gaining new authority.
Execution ↔ ValidationExecution generates evidence; independent Validation judges it.
Human Owner ↔ every stageHuman judgment is concentrated at intent, tradeoff, approval, change, and acceptance points.
The architecture is not trying to make AI less autonomous. It is trying to make greater autonomy safe to delegate.
