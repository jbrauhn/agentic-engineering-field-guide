Capability Interfaces

Agents need to act in real systems, but the AE method should not depend on whichever vendor happens to provide those systems today.

What it is

Open capability interfaces into the organization’s ecosystem.

Why it matters

Tools and vendors change faster than engineering principles.

Failure prevented

Vendor-shaped architecture and brittle integrations.

Core philosophy

Open interfaces; organizational tool choice.

Direct product access, APIs, Model Context Protocol (MCP), enterprise brokers, and custom adapters can all be valid. The organization chooses the integration pattern during installation; the AE system remains portable.

Planning

Identifies the capabilities the work needs.

Governance

Scopes which actions are available to this actor and task.

Execution

Uses tools; results become observations and evidence.
